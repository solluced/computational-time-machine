# Computational Time Machine (CTM) v2.0 — Technical Preprint

**Author:** Nickolay Gorlanov (Nick G.)  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22689836  
**Zenodo:** https://zenodo.org/records/22689836

Version 2.0 develops CTM into a falsifiable measurement and experimental framework for generational AI R&D systems.

It formalizes the Technological Time Compression Ratio (TTCR), introduces Causal Inheritance Depth (CID), distinguishes verified inheritance from ordinary parallel-agent scaling, defines matched experimental baselines and ablations, and adds explicit constraints for compute, energy, physical experimentation, provenance, independent verification, safety, and falsification.
