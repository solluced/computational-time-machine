# CTM v2.0 — Changes from v1.0

Version 2.0 expands the original four-page CTM concept into a technical, falsifiable experimental framework.

## Major additions

- Reframes CTM as an independent research preprint rather than an OpenAI submission.
- Adds structured related work covering automated scientific agents, evaluator-driven discovery, self-improving agents, and self-driving laboratories.
- Formalizes Civilization-in-the-Loop as a generational state-transition system with controlled inheritance and promotion gates.
- Refines TTCR into a domain-specific ratio of externally validated progress rates against a matched reference R&D system.
- Introduces Causal Inheritance Depth (CID) plus metrics for replication, promotion precision, inheritance uplift, reuse, physical closure, progress per joule/dollar, oversight load, and safety interventions.
- Defines controlled experimental arms and ablations that isolate generational inheritance from shared memory, branching, and ordinary parallelism.
- Adds a concrete 30-day first-demonstration protocol with pre-registration, hidden evaluation, multiple seeds, provenance, and replay requirements.
- Adds explicit compute, energy, coordination, physical-latency, economic, verification, provenance, and safety constraints.
- Adds failure modes, falsification criteria, and a staged path from digital CTM to bounded physical closure.
